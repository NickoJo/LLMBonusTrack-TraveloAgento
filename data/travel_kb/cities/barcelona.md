# Barcelona — Travel Guide

## General Information

Barcelona is the capital of Catalonia, Spain's most-visited city with over 9 million tourists annually. A blend of Gothic architecture, Modernisme, world-class beaches, and vibrant nightlife. Population 1.6 million. Best Mediterranean climate: warm summers, mild winters.

## Top Attractions

1. **Sagrada Família** — Gaudí's unfinished masterpiece basilica. Book tickets online (€26–€36), entry queues can be 2+ hours without booking. Open daily 9:00–20:00 (summer).
2. **Park Güell** — UNESCO-listed park with Gaudí mosaics and city panoramas. Timed tickets required for monumental zone (€10). Free zones available.
3. **Las Ramblas** — Barcelona's iconic pedestrian boulevard. Beware pickpockets. Runs 1.2 km from Plaça de Catalunya to the sea.
4. **Gothic Quarter (Barri Gòtic)** — Labyrinthine medieval streets with Roman ruins, Barcelona Cathedral (free), and tapas bars.
5. **Camp Nou** — FC Barcelona's stadium, Europe's largest. Museum tour available (€28). Book in advance on match days.
6. **Barceloneta Beach** — The main city beach, 1.1 km long. Gets crowded July–August. Best visited before 11:00 or after 18:00.
7. **La Boqueria Market** — Famous covered market on Las Ramblas. Best visited 9:00–11:00 to avoid crowds. Fresh fruit, seafood, local products.
8. **Casa Batlló** — Gaudí's architectural gem on Passeig de Gràcia (€29–€39). Evening "Magic Nights" show highly recommended.
9. **Montjuïc** — Hill with castle, gardens, and Olympic stadium. Cable car available. Great sunset views.
10. **El Born neighbourhood** — Trendy area with Picasso Museum (€14), boutiques, and craft cocktail bars.

## Restaurants and Cafés

### Budget (under €15/person)
- **Bar Cañete** — Excellent pintxos and tapas near Las Ramblas. Arrive before 13:00 or expect a queue.
- **La Pepita** — Sandwiches ("montaditos") from €2. Always busy, worth the wait. C/ Conde del Asalto 35.
- **Cervecería Catalana** — Great tapas and beer at moderate prices. Carrer de Mallorca 236.

### Mid-range (€15–€40/person)
- **Tickets** — Albert Adrià's tapas bar (Ferran Adrià's brother). Reservations required months in advance. Check website for cancellations.
- **Bar del Pla** — Modern Catalan cuisine in Born. Friendly staff, solid wine list.
- **La Mar Salada** — Seafood with sea views near Barceloneta. Paella and fideuà are standouts.

### Premium (€40+/person)
- **Disfrutar** — World's #2 restaurant (2024). Avant-garde cuisine. Book 2–3 months ahead.
- **ABaC** — 3 Michelin stars. One of Spain's finest dining experiences.

## Transport

- **Airport El Prat** — 20 km from centre. Aerobús to Plaça Catalunya: €6.75, 35 min. Taxi: €35–€45.
- **Metro** — Extensive network, T-Casual 10-trip card: €11.35. Runs 05:00–00:00 (Fri/Sat until 02:00).
- **Bike** — Bicing city bikes (subscription) or tourist rentals (~€15/day). Flat city centre, hills elsewhere.
- **Bus Turístic** — Hop-on hop-off tourist bus covering all major attractions (€30/day).

## Tips and Warnings

- **Best months**: May–June and September–October. July–August is scorching (35°C+) and extremely crowded.
- **Pickpockets**: High risk on Las Ramblas and Metro L3. Use a money belt or front pocket.
- **Meal times**: Lunch 14:00–16:00, dinner 21:00–23:00. Restaurants before 20:30 are tourist traps.
- **Siesta**: Some smaller shops close 14:00–17:00.
- **Language**: Catalan and Spanish both official. English widely understood in tourist areas.
- **Tipping**: Not mandatory, 5–10% appreciated in restaurants.
- **Beach safety**: No diving from jetties; lifeguards on duty 10:00–19:00 Jun–Sep.
