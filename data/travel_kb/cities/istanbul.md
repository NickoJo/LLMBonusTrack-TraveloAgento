# Istanbul — Travel Guide

## General Information

Istanbul is the only city in the world spanning two continents (Europe and Asia). Former capital of Byzantine and Ottoman Empires. Population 15 million. Transcontinental bridge between East and West — unique culture, cuisine, and architecture. Currency: Turkish Lira (TRY). English widely spoken in tourist areas.

## Top Attractions

1. **Hagia Sophia (Ayasofya)** — 1500-year-old cathedral turned mosque. Free entry, modest dress required (headscarf for women). Open daily except during prayer times. Arrive early to avoid queues.
2. **Topkapi Palace** — Ottoman sultans' palace with Harem (separate ticket), treasury, and Bosphorus views. €15 + €15 Harem. Closed Tuesdays.
3. **Blue Mosque (Sultan Ahmed)** — 17th-century mosque with six minarets. Free entry, closed during prayer. Dress modestly, remove shoes.
4. **Grand Bazaar (Kapalıçarşı)** — One of the world's oldest and largest covered markets, 4,000+ shops. Best for spices, leather, ceramics, textiles. Open Mon–Sat 8:30–19:00.
5. **Spice Bazaar (Mısır Çarşısı)** — More manageable than Grand Bazaar. Excellent for saffron, Turkish tea, lokum. Near Eminönü waterfront.
6. **Bosphorus Cruise** — Ferry from Eminönü (public ferry, ~€3) or private cruise. Best sunset views of palaces and bridges. Highly recommended.
7. **Dolmabahçe Palace** — 19th-century Ottoman palace on the European Bosphorus shore. Ornate interior, 285 rooms. Guided tour only. Closed Mondays.
8. **Galata Tower** — Medieval tower with panoramic city views. Entry €7. Queues at sunset — book online.
9. **Istiklal Avenue** — Istanbul's main pedestrian street (1.4 km), tram runs through it. Shops, cafés, galleries, nightlife.
10. **Princes' Islands (Adalar)** — Car-free islands in Sea of Marmara. Ferry from Kabataş (~45 min). Rent bikes or horse carriages. Perfect half-day escape.

## Restaurants and Food

### Street Food and Budget (under ₺200 / ~€6)
- **Simit** — Sesame-crusted bread ring, sold on every corner. 5–10 ₺.
- **Balık Ekmek (Fish Sandwich)** — Grilled mackerel in bread at Eminönü docks. 50–70 ₺. Iconic Istanbul street food.
- **Döner kebab** — Best from local shops away from tourist streets. 80–120 ₺.
- **Lokanta (canteen-style)** — Self-service traditional food, excellent value. Look for lines of locals.

### Mid-range (€10–€25/person)
- **Tarihi Sultanahmet Köftecisi** — Legendary köfte restaurant near Blue Mosque since 1920. Queue is normal, worth it.
- **Çiya Sofrası (Kadıköy)** — Best traditional Anatolian food, Asian side. Worth the ferry. Lunch only.
- **Karaköy Güllüoğlu** — Istanbul's finest baklava. Take a box home. Karaköy neighbourhood.

### Premium (€25+/person)
- **Mikla** — Rooftop restaurant, panoramic Bosphorus views, modern Turkish cuisine. Book well ahead.
- **Neolokal** — Istanbul Modern restaurant, innovative Ottoman cuisine revived.

## Transport

- **Istanbul Airport (IST)** — 35–45 km from city centre. Metro M11 to Gayrettepe: €2, 35 min. Taxi: €25–€40 (use official taxi or BiTaksi app).
- **Metro and Tram** — Extensive network. Istanbulkart (travel card) required: €2 deposit + top-up. Single journey ~€0.50.
- **Ferry** — Essential and scenic. Crosses Bosphorus Eminönü↔Kadıköy: €0.60. Public ferries are the local experience.
- **Taxis** — Use meter or apps (BiTaksi). Avoid unlicensed cabs. Agree price beforehand if no meter.

## Tips

- **Best months**: April–May and September–October. Avoid August (35°C+ heat and crowds).
- **Dress code**: Cover shoulders and knees for mosques. Bring/buy a headscarf at Hagia Sophia entrance.
- **Bargaining**: Expected at Grand Bazaar and Spice Bazaar. Start at 40–50% of asking price.
- **Safety**: Generally safe for tourists. Beware tourist scams near Grand Bazaar (fake "guides").
- **Turkish tea (çay)**: Always free when browsing shops — a cultural tradition, not an obligation to buy.
- **Currency**: TRY (Turkish Lira). USD/EUR widely accepted in tourist shops but TRY gives better rates.
- **Call to prayer (ezan)**: 5 times daily from minarets. Embrace it — it's part of the Istanbul experience.
