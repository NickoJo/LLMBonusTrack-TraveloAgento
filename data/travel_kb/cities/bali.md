# Bali — Travel Guide

## General Information

Bali is Indonesia's most famous island — "Island of the Gods". Known for Hindu temples, terraced rice fields, world-class surf, and wellness retreats. Population 4.2 million. Main tourist areas: Seminyak/Kuta (beach/party), Ubud (culture/nature), Uluwatu (luxury/surf), Canggu (digital nomads). Currency: Indonesian Rupiah (IDR). 1 USD ≈ 15,700 IDR. ATMs widespread.

## Top Attractions

1. **Tanah Lot Temple** — Iconic sea temple on a rock, silhouetted at sunset. Free to view from shore, IDR 60,000 to enter grounds. Best visited 17:00–19:00 for sunset.
2. **Tegallalang Rice Terraces** — UNESCO-listed cascading rice paddies near Ubud. Entry IDR 50,000. Swing/photo spots IDR 50,000–150,000 extra. Arrive before 9:00 to beat crowds.
3. **Ubud Monkey Forest** — 700+ long-tailed macaques in sacred forest. Entry IDR 80,000. Keep bags closed, remove glasses. Open 8:30–18:00.
4. **Uluwatu Temple** — Clifftop temple with dramatic ocean views. Kecak Fire Dance at sunset (IDR 150,000) — unmissable. Sarong required.
5. **Seminyak & Kuta Beach** — Prime surf beaches. Surf lessons from IDR 200,000. Sunset at Ku De Ta beach club is legendary.
6. **Mount Batur (Gunung Batur)** — Active volcano, pre-dawn trek (4:00 AM start). Guides: IDR 300,000–500,000. Rewarded with sunrise above the clouds.
7. **Tirta Gangga Water Palace** — Royal water palace with lotus ponds and stepping stones. Entry IDR 50,000. Serene and photogenic.
8. **Nusa Penida Island** — Dramatic cliffs (Kelingking Beach), crystal water. Fast boat from Sanur: IDR 150,000 one-way. Full day excursion recommended.
9. **Ubud Art Market (Pasar Seni)** — Batik, wood carvings, silver jewellery. Bargain hard. Morning is best before tourist rush.
10. **Besakih "Mother Temple"** — Bali's holiest temple complex on Mount Agung slopes. IDR 60,000 + guide (mandatory). Dress code strictly enforced.

## Restaurants and Food

### Local Warung / Budget (under IDR 50,000 / ~€3)
- **Nasi goreng** — Indonesian fried rice with egg. Available everywhere, IDR 15,000–30,000. The definitive Bali breakfast.
- **Mie goreng** — Fried noodles, equally ubiquitous and delicious.
- **Babi guling (suckling pig)** — Balinese specialty. Best at Ibu Oka in Ubud (arrive early, sells out by noon).
- **Warung Babi Guling** — Local canteen-style, queue of locals is a good sign.

### Mid-range (IDR 100,000–300,000 / €6–€20 per person)
- **Locavore (Ubud)** — Top Indonesian restaurant, farm-to-table philosophy. Reservations weeks ahead required.
- **Watercress Café (Ubud)** — Excellent Indonesian and Western fusion, lovely garden setting.
- **Mama San (Seminyak)** — Southeast Asian street food elevated. Busy, vibrant atmosphere.

### Premium (IDR 500,000+ / €30+/person)
- **Swept Away (COMO Uma Ubud)** — Riverside fine dining amidst the jungle. Romantic and beautiful.
- **Ku De Ta (Seminyak)** — Beachfront, great cocktails and sunsets, upscale Asian cuisine.

## Transport

- **Ngurah Rai Airport (DPS)** — Denpasar, 12 km from Seminyak. Official taxi: IDR 100,000–200,000 to main areas. Grab app is cheaper.
- **Grab** — The essential app in Bali. Reliable, transparent pricing, accepted everywhere except near tourist traps where drivers block it.
- **Scooter rental** — IDR 60,000–100,000/day. International licence required technically; helmets mandatory. Not recommended in heavy traffic for inexperienced riders.
- **Driver hire** — IDR 400,000–600,000 for a full day with driver. Highly recommended for day trips to temples.
- **Between areas**: Ubud to Seminyak ~1.5 hrs. No reliable public transport — hire a driver or Grab.

## Tips

- **Best months**: April–October (dry season). November–March is wet season with daily rain.
- **Temple etiquette**: Sarong required at all temples (often rentable at gate). Cover knees and shoulders.
- **Offering awareness**: Small palm-leaf offerings (canang sari) are everywhere on pavements — step around, not over them.
- **Drinking water**: Never drink tap water. Bottled water IDR 5,000–10,000. Many hotels provide refill stations.
- **Bargaining**: Expected at markets. Offer 30–50% of asking price, be polite and smile.
- **Monkeys**: Aggressive at monkey forest and some temples. Keep food/glasses/bags concealed.
- **Surf**: Kuta good for beginners, Uluwatu and Padang Padang for experienced surfers only.
- **Currency**: Withdraw IDR from ATMs (fees apply). Avoid money changers on the street — use authorised booths or banks.
- **Visa**: Most nationalities get 30-day visa on arrival (free); extendable online for 30 more days.
